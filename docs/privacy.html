Privacy Policy

Baanto AI

Effective date: 19 November 2026

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Baanto, we may process your name, phone number, email address (if provided), account type, city and address details; food listing information, descriptions, pickup and expiry details, and photos you upload; location information such as selected city or coordinates; food requests, request status, chat messages, reviews, reports and device push-notification tokens. We also receive basic technical and authentication information needed to operate and protect the app.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, display and manage food listings, help people discover nearby surplus food, coordinate requests and pickups, provide chat and notifications, handle reviews and reports, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, maintain and improve Baanto, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Location and food photos

Location features may use a city you select or location permissions and coordinates you choose to provide. Your precise pickup point may be shared with people involved in a relevant pickup so they can find the food. Food photos are stored and may be displayed with the related listing. Do not upload photos or location details you do not want shared with other users.

4. Phone number and communications

Your phone number is not intended to be publicly displayed on food cards. A donor’s phone number is shared with a requester only when the donor chooses to share it for an accepted request. Baanto may use an OTP provider, including MSG91, to verify phone numbers. Push notifications may be delivered through Expo and relevant mobile operating-system services.

5. When information is shared

We share listing and profile information with other users as needed to operate the community marketplace. Request details and chat content are visible to the people participating in that request. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate authentication, database and file storage, maps or place search, OTP verification, and push notifications, which may include Supabase, Google, MSG91 and Expo. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, Baanto and the public. We do not sell personal information.

6. Storage, security and retention

Information is stored using the services that power Baanto. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs and comply with law. When you delete your account, Baanto is designed to delete your account and associated listings, requests, chats, reviews, reports, saved listing photos and sign-in access. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where legally required.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can manage certain profile and location details in the app and can stop using device location permissions through your device settings. You can request permanent account deletion from Settings & Privacy → Delete account. Deletion is intended to be permanent and cannot be undone. If you need help, contact baanto.ai@gmail.com.

8. Children and changes

Baanto is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter. We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the updated policy and revise the effective date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email baanto.ai@gmail.com.
